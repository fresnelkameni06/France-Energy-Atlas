**00:30–01:00** — Show the sidebar filters (year, date, region, indicator, month, energy source) and the KPI banner.

**01:00–01:45** — Demonstrate the regional comparison, map and generation mix; explain one actual numerical finding shown by the dashboard.

**01:45–02:25** — Show temporal heatmap, seasonal variability and Pearson relationships, explicitly distinguishing association from causation.

**02:25–03:00** — Show preprocessing evidence, completeness and 2026 year-to-date limitation.

**03:00–03:30** — Present conclusions and the reproducibility/Excel refresh workflow.
