**Narrative design rules**

Every analysis page starts with one question and interprets one chart or evidence table. It provides axes, units, interactive tooltips and at least one caveat or next step. The concluding page synthesizes insights calculated from user-selected data. No synthetic numbers or unsupported causes are presented.
